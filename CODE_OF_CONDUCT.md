# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone other people can work with. Technical disagreement is welcome;
contempt is not.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the code, the design, or the claim — not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person is competent and working with different information.
  Ask what you are missing before concluding they are wrong.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that is technically fine but not
  a fit for the project, and say so with a reason.
- Respect the time of whoever reads your issue: include the version, the
  platform, and what you actually ran.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in the repository or anywhere the
  project is discussed.
- Discrimination or derogatory remarks based on race, ethnicity, nationality,
  religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or
  appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions to relitigate them, or
  demanding unpaid work from maintainers.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages, discussions,
and any space where you are participating as a contributor to this project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially; do not include credentials or third-party personal data.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, and
block accounts, with a warning first where the situation allows one and without
one where it does not.
